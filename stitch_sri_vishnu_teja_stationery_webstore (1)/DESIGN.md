## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a calm, premium, paper-like tactile aesthetic tailored for stationery retail, print-on-demand services, and local counter operations. It balances the timeless serenity of physical books and fine stationery with the crisp clarity of a modern digital commerce platform.

### Design Movement & Mood
- **Tactile Paper Skeuomorphism Meets Modern Utility**: Drawing inspiration from artisanal paper textures, pressed card stock, desk timbers, and fountain pen ink, the interface feels rooted in physical craftsmanship. Elements sit on soft multi-toned surfaces rather than harsh high-tech canvases.
- **Atmosphere**: Academic, thoughtful, welcoming, and orderly. It replaces cluttered retail noise with focused whitespace, calm muted tints, and deliberate structural anchors.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Trustworthy, approachable for bilingual multi-generational customers, yet remarkably polished for administrative and counter workflows.

## Colors

The color system operates on an intentional **60-30-5-5 surface distribution**:
- **60% Base Surface (`#FFFFFF` Paper White)**: Uncluttered, crisp paper canvas providing readability and airy negative space.
- **30% Soft Containers (`#F6F5F2` Soft Paper & `#F7EFE3` Warm Cream)**: Calibrated surface tiers for cards, 3D model staging grounds, and promotional callouts.
- **5% Structural Anchors (`#1F2640` Navy Ink)**: Directing eye flow through top utility bars, primary interactive buttons, headings, and selected states.
- **5% Accents & Semantics**: Soft Saffron (`#EDC27E`) grounds 3D podiums, while vivid Saffron (`#E8952B`) is restricted to tiny, high-attention cues (360° product badges, low-stock alerts, active order pings).

### Category Tint Archetypes
Category discovery tiles and collection tags use muted, archival pigments. Neon and high-chroma saturated hues are strictly prohibited across background surfaces to preserve the natural paper palette.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites geometric, editorial authority with effortless tabular legibility.

- **Headlines & Display**: Set in `Epilogue` (or bold geometric sans `Poppins`), providing high impact, sturdy letterforms reminiscent of architectural signage and hot-metal book printing.
- **Body & Controls**: Set in `Inter`, optimized for small sizes, transaction numbers, specs, and form controls.
- **Multilingual Support**: Pair with `Noto Sans` (specifically Telugu localized glyphs) for bilingual UI ribbons, Telugu category translations, and localized item titles. Telugu text is styled at identical optical heights, using medium weights (`500`) to guarantee legibility alongside Latin script.

## Layout & Spacing

The system enforces a clean 12-column grid system for desktop interfaces and an adaptive 4-column structure on mobile devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px - 1440px)**: 12-column fluid grid, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, 40px (`2.5rem`) outer margin. Product catalogs display in a 4-column grid. Admin navigation sits in a fixed 260px sidebar.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, 16px gutters, 24px outer margin. Catalogs reflow into 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (360px - 767px)**: 4-column grid, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) outer margin. Touch targets maintain a minimum dimension of 48px to 52px for rapid counter actions.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component spacing follows an 8pt architectural rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than high-contrast digital dropshadows, visual depth is achieved through layered paper surfaces, subtle contact occlusion, and soft tinted boundaries.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (Base)**: `#FFFFFF` Paper White serves as the background plane.
2. **Resting Stage Layer**: `#F6F5F2` Soft Paper surfaces layered directly on top with a 1px solid stroke of `#E7E3DC`. Shadows are omitted at rest to maintain a flat, structured paper quality.
3. **Lifted Objects & Interactive Hover**: When a card is hovered or focused, an ambient contact shadow appears: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 38, 64, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 38, 64, 0.04)`.
4. **3D Interactive Pedestals**: 3D stationery items sit upon `#EDC27E` Soft Saffron geometric pedestals. The pedestal projects an elliptical contact shadow directly onto the floor plane: `radial-gradient(ellipse at center, rgba(31, 38, 64, 0.15) 0%, rgba(31, 38, 64, 0) 70%)`.
5. **Modals & Slide-out Drawers**: Drawers in the Counter App slide in from the right edge with a soft layered shadow (`-12px 0 32px rgba(31, 38, 64, 0.08)`) over a 40% Navy Ink scrim (`rgba(31, 38, 64, 0.40)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances organic card corners with classic pill buttons:
- **Product & Content Cards**: Roundedness level 2 (`12px` on high-density administrative cards, `16px` on customer-facing product stages and counter touchscreens).
- **Interactive Action Pills**: Fully rounded pill radius (`9999px`) for primary CTAs, search bars, category discovery pills, and counter status badges.
- **Pedestal Form Factor**: 3D display podiums adopt cylindrical or beveled squircle volumes, evoking physical wooden or paperboard display blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Full pill geometry (`9999px`), `#1F2640` Navy Ink background, `#FFFFFF` text, with `16px` horizontal padding. On hover, background shifts to `#2A3356`. Height is 48px on desktop and 52px on mobile touch views.
- **Secondary Action**: Pill shape, `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px `#1F2640` border, `#1F2640` text.
- **Accent Action**: Used exclusively for primary success checkpoints (e.g., "Track Order" or "Mark Paid"), colored in `#E8952B` with crisp white text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Pills**: Soft Paper `#F6F5F2` fill, 1px `#E7E3DC` border, `#1B1F2A` text. Active state transitions to `#1F2640` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **360° Model Badge**: Positioned top-left on 3D stages. Pill container with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E7E3DC` border, carrying a tiny `#E8952B` rotation glyph and 11px uppercase label.
- **Stock Alert Chips**:
  - In Stock: `#EAF6EE` background, `#2F8F5B` text.
  - Low Stock: `#FEF4E8` background, `#E8952B` text.
  - Out of Stock: `#FCEEEF` background, `#C0453A` text.

### Cards & 3D Stages
- **Product Card**: Constructed on `#F6F5F2` with a 1px `#E7E3DC` border. The upper 65% contains the interactive WebGL viewport where the 3D model hovers over a `#EDC27E` pedestal.
- **Card Footer**: Contains the product title in `#1B1F2A`, bilingual sub-label in `#6B7280`, unit price, and an inline circular add-to-cart button.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Search Fields**: Minimum 48px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E7E3DC` border, 12px corner radius. Placeholder text rendered in `#6B7280`. Focused state features an intentional 1.5px `#1F2640` border without heavy color glows.

### Lists & Tables
- **Counter Ledger & Orders**: Table headers in `#F6F5F2` with 11px uppercase muted labels. Row items separated by 1px `#E7E3DC` horizontal dividers. Pending orders feature a prominent 3px left border in Saffron `#E8952B`.
